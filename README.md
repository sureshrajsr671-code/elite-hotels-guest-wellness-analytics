# Elite Hotels International: Guest Health & Wellness Diagnostics

Cardiovascular Risk Profiling Across 1,025 Guest Profiles to Power Personalized Wellness Retreats & Preventive Hospitality Services.

---

## 📌 Executive Diagnostics & Overview
Is clinical-hospitality investigation me Elite Hotels International ke **1,025 guest health evaluation records** ka in-depth analysis kiya gaya hai taaki preventive cardiovascular diagnostics ke jariye tailored wellness retreats aur high-margin longevity tourism ko develop kiya ja sake[cite: 19].

### Key Clinical Benchmarks:
- **Total Analyzed Profiles:** 1,025 guest clinical records[cite: 19]
- **High Cardiac Risk Share:** **51.3%** (526 guests elevated cardiovascular risk indicators exhibit karte hain)[cite: 19]
- **Serum Cholesterol Level:** Evaluated guests ka average cholesterol **246 mg/dL** paya gaya (normal 200 mg/dL limit se kaafi zyada)[cite: 19]
- **Young Cohort Risk (< 45 Yrs):** Sabse bada surprise finding ye raha ki younger evaluating cohorts me **72.4% risk share** darj hua, jo highly reversible lifestyle factors ko darshata hai[cite: 19].

---

## 📊 Dataset & Biomarker Architecture
- **Multi-Variate Clinical Tracking:** 1,025 health records tracking 14 physiological biomarkers: Age, Sex, Resting Blood Pressure (`trestbps`), Serum Cholesterol (`chol`), Fasting Blood Sugar (`fbs`), Resting ECG (`restecg`), Maximum Heart Rate (`thalach`), Exercise-Induced Angina (`exang`), and ST Depression (`oldpeak`)[cite: 19].
- **Symptom Stratification:** Chest pain types (Typical Angina, Atypical Angina, Non-Anginal, Asymptomatic)[cite: 19].
- **Preventive Objective:** Clinical data ko luxury itineraries, supervised workouts, aur heart-healthy low-sodium dining me convert karna[cite: 19].

---

## 🔍 Core Physiological Catalysts & Clinical Findings

### 1. Chest Pain & Symptom Stratification
- **Atypical & Non-Anginal Discomfort:** Atypical Angina (CP=1) aur Non-Anginal Pain (CP=2) report karne wale guests **75% se zyada correlation** show karte hain elevated cardiac risk ke sath[cite: 19]. Inhe targeted biofeedback aur stress-reduction therapy ki zaroorat hoti hai[cite: 19].
- **Asymptomatic Risk (CP=3):** Asymptomatic guests me silent cardiovascular strain paya gaya, jise arrival check-ups aur resting ECG ke bina detect nahi kiya ja sakta[cite: 19].

### 2. Physical Capacity & Exercise-Induced Angina (Exang)
- **Exercise Angina Impact:** Exercise-induced angina (Exang=1) wale guests exertional stress ke dauran suppressed maximum heart rate (avg **132 bpm**) aur elevated ST depression show karte hain[cite: 19]. Inhe high-intensity training ke bajaye supervised hydrotherapy aur gentle mobility ki zaroorat hoti hai[cite: 19].
- **Heart Rate Reserve (Thalach):** 150 bpm se upar max heart rate achieve karne wale guests strong positive cardiovascular response demonstrate karte hain[cite: 19].

### 3. Hypercholesterolemia & Blood Pressure
- High baseline cholesterol (avg **246 mg/dL**) aur resting blood pressure (> 135 mm Hg in 50+ age groups) vascular stiffness aur plaque formation ke risk ko amplify karte hain[cite: 19].

## 🛠️ Wellness Program Rollout Roadmap & Action Pillars

- **Q1 - Screening Integration:** Check-in ke dauran voluntary non-invasive arrival biomarker check-ups aur resting ECG screening kiosks deploy karna[cite: 19].
- **Q2 - Dietary Overhaul:** Professional clinical nutritionists dwara curated low-sodium, plant-based aur antioxidant anti-inflammatory gourmet meal programs roll out karna[cite: 19].
- **Q3 - Supervised Cardiac Fitness:** Exercise-induced angina aur heart rate reserve ke hisab se customized, heart-rate monitored training sessions conduct karna[cite: 19].
- **Q4 - Post-Stay Health Telemetry:** Checkout ke baad hospital-grade smartwatch telemetry aur virtual cardiologist consultations provide karke brand stickiness aur repeat visits ensure karna[cite: 19].
