# Elite Hotels International: Guest Health and Wellness Diagnostics

Cardiovascular Risk Profiling Across 1,025 Guest Profiles to Power Personalized Wellness Retreats and Preventive Hospitality Services.

---

## Executive Diagnostics and Overview
An empirical clinical investigation across 1,025 diagnostic guest records evaluating key physiological biomarkers to develop medical hospitality and preventative wellness packages.

### Key Clinical Benchmarks
- Total Analyzed Profiles: 1,025 guest clinical records
- High Cardiac Risk Prevalence: 51.3% (526 guests exhibit elevated cardiovascular risk indicators)
- Average Serum Cholesterol: 246 mg/dL across evaluated profiles (above the 200 mg/dL clinical threshold)
- Young Cohort Risk: Younger evaluated guests (< 45 years) demonstrate a 72.4% risk share, driven primarily by reversible lifestyle and stress factors.

---

## Dataset and Biomarker Architecture
- Evaluated Biomarkers: Age, sex, resting blood pressure (trestbps), serum cholesterol (chol), resting ECG (restecg), max heart rate (thalach), exercise-induced angina (exang), and ST depression (oldpeak).
- Clinical Stratification: Categorizing symptoms into Typical Angina, Atypical Angina, Non-Anginal Pain, and Asymptomatic presentations.

---

## Core Physiological Risk Catalysts

### 1. Chest Pain and Symptom Correlations
- Guests reporting Atypical Angina (CP=1) and Non-Anginal Pain (CP=2) show over 75% correlation with cardiovascular risk.
- Asymptomatic profiles (CP=3) frequently conceal silent cardiac strain that requires on-site diagnostic screening.

### 2. Exertion and Physical Capacity
- Guests positive for exercise-induced angina (Exang=1) exhibit lower maximum heart rate capacity (avg 132 bpm) and elevated ST depression.
- These guests require low-impact, supervised physical therapy and hydrotherapy over intensive workouts.

### 3. Hypercholesterolemia and Blood Pressure
- High average cholesterol levels (246 mg/dL) combined with elevated resting blood pressure (> 135 mm Hg in older cohorts) highlight the necessity of plant-based culinary programs.

## Wellness Program Rollout Roadmap

- Q1 - Screening Integration: Deploy voluntary arrival biomarker check-ups and resting ECG screenings at check-in.
- Q2 - Dietary Overhaul: Introduce heart-healthy, low-sodium culinary packages crafted by nutritionists.
- Q3 - Supervised Fitness: Launch customized cardiac fitness coaching tailored to exercise-induced angina and heart rate reserves.
- Q4 - Post-Stay Telemetry: Provide continuous smartwatch health telemetry and virtual doctor consultations post-checkout.
